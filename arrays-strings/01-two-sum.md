## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)
**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach
I used a brute-force approach with nested loops to iterate through the array, checking every possible pair of numbers until finding the two that add up to the target sum.

### Complexity
- Time: $O(n^2)$
- Space: $O(1)$

### Notes
While the nested loop is easy to implement, a hash map approach would reduce the time complexity to $O(n)$.
